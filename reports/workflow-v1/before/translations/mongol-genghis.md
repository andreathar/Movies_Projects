# Genghis Khan — Historical Language Translation (Middle Mongol)

**Edition:** Edition H (Reviewed Historical-Language Edition)  
**Language Target:** 13th-Century Middle Mongol (*Dumda-du Monggol Kele*) Reconstruction  
**Register:** Steppe nobility / Imperial court register (Secret History of the Mongols style)

---

## 1. Dialogue Script & Phonetic Transcription

### Line K-L01
- **Speaker:** Khan (Off-Screen)
- **English Original:** *"Will my sons keep what I have made?"*
- **Middle Mongol Reconstruction:** **"Minu kö'ütd minu bayiguγsan ulus-i sakiqu buyu?"**
- **IPA Phonetics:** /mi.nu kœːtd mi.nu ba.ji.ɢʊʁ.san ʊ.lʊs.i sa.xi.qʊ bu.ju/
- **Phonetic Guide:** *Mee-noo koo-ootd mee-noo bah-yee-googh-sahn oo-loos-ee sah-khee-koo boo-yoo?*
- **Literal Gloss:** My sons my established nation-(accusative) guard will?

---

### Line K-L02
- **Speaker:** Andre (Seated, Facing Camera-Right)
- **English Original:** *"They will enlarge it. Routes across Eurasia will carry merchants, ideas, and knowledge. But conquest also leaves ruined cities and families who never return."*
- **Middle Mongol Reconstruction:** **"Tede yeke-ber öskekü boluyu. Qorin ayan-u mör-iyer qudaldučin, sedkil, erdem tegegegdeküy-e. Ketürkei dayin ber ebderkei balγad, büri-dü ülü harilqu ger-ün ulus-i üldegemüy."**
- **IPA Phonetics:** /te.de je.xe.ber œs.xe.xu bo.lu.ju. qo.rin a.jan.u mœr.i.jer qu.dal.du.tʃin sed.xil er.dem te.geːg.dex.yj.e. xe.tœr.xei da.jin ber eb.der.xei bal.ʁad by.ri.dy y.ly ha.ril.qu ger.yn u.lus.i yl.de.ge.myj/
- **Phonetic Guide:** *TEH-deh YEH-kheh-behr OOS-kheh-khoo boh-LOO-yoo. Khoh-reen ah-YAHN-oo MOOR-ee-yehr khoo-dahl-DOO-cheen, sehd-KEEL, EHR-dehm teh-GAYG-dehk-yoo-eh. Kheh-TOOR-kay DAH-yeen behr ehb-dehr-KAY BAHL-ghahd, boo-ree-DOO oo-LOO hah-REEL-khoo GEHR-oon OO-loos-ee ool-deh-geh-MOO-yee.*
- **Literal Gloss:** They greater make shall. Across long road-routes merchants, thought, knowledge carried-shall-be. Excessive warfare however ruined cities, forever not returning home-folk leaves.

### Line K-L03
- **Speaker:** Khan (Off-Screen)
- **English Original:** *"And what will they call me?"*
- **Middle Mongol Reconstruction:** **"Namayi keni kemen neregüy-e?"**
- **IPA Phonetics:** /na.ma.ji xe.ni xe.men ne.re.gyj.e/
- **Phonetic Guide:** *Nah-mah-yee KHEH-nee kheh-mehn neh-reh-GOO-yoo-eh?*
- **Literal Gloss:** Me who saying will-they-name?

### Line K-L04
- **Speaker:** Andre (Seated & Audio Overlap on Table Cut)
- **English Original:** *"A founder. A conqueror. They will argue about what your empire joined—and what it destroyed. None of those words can make the dead speak."*
- **Middle Mongol Reconstruction:** **"Bayiγuluγsan kü'ün. Ezilegči. Ulus čin-u yaghu-yi qolboba, yaghu-yi ebdeb kemen margalduqu bui. Tegebed ber terigün üges ükegsed-i ügülügüljü ülü čidayu."**
- **IPA Phonetics:** /ba.ji.ʁu.luʁ.san xyːn. e.zi.leg.tʃi. u.lus tʃin.u ja.ʁu.ji qol.bo.ba ja.ʁu.ji eb.deb xe.men mar.gal.du.qu bui. te.ge.bed ber te.ri.gyn y.ges y.xeg.sed.i y.gy.ly.gyl.dʒy y.ly tʃi.da.ju/
- **Phonetic Guide:** *Bah-yee-ghoo-loogh-sahn KHOON. Eh-zee-lehg-CHEE. Oo-loos cheen-oo YAH-ghoo-yee khol-BOH-bah, YAH-ghoo-yee ehb-DEHB kheh-mehn mahr-gahl-DOO-khoo booy. Teh-geh-behd behr teh-ree-GOON OO-gehs oo-khehg-SEHD-ee oo-goo-loo-gool-JOO oo-LOO chee-DAH-yoo.*
- **Literal Gloss:** Founding person. Conqueror. Nation your what connected, what destroyed saying they-dispute. Even so those words dead-ones to-speak not can.

## 2. Dubbing & Lip-Sync Editorial Notes

- Middle Mongol features guttural consonants (`/q/`, `/ʁ/`, `/x/`) and front-rounded vowels (`/œ/`, `/y/`).
- Delivery should be deep, resonant, and deliberate.
