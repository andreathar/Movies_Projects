## 6. Five-encounter shot and sound acceptance checklist

Apply to each selected shot, not automatically to every study/slot. The 41-slot plan is coverage accounting, not 41 paid job orders. Five kinds of detail studies are alternatives, not an obligatory repeated montage.

1. Source/intent: person, fictional setting/date, canonical line ID, what the figure wants, what Andre personally wants to tell them, prior emotional state, difficult word/idea, visible response, consequence for Andre.
2. Historical discipline: fact source versus inferred/fictional design; selected footwear/floor/manga/hair/objects; no full contemporary actor likeness; no unsupported illness/measurements/equipment; uncertain Arcetri floor/Longwood dimensions explicit.
3. Identity packet: correct real versus synthetic category; D01/D02/D03; Element if supported; exact selected file hashes and shot-specific likeness approval, no automatic inheritance from pilots.
4. Shared space: table anchor D04 with ring/chip; seat and table scale; Andre left/camera-right gaze D09; selected shoulder/eyeline; changing camera side requires a designed re-establishment, not mirrored imagery.
5. Face safety: historical head/crop margin for intended motion; rear hair does not license profile/cheek/ear reveal; eyes-only crop covers actual intermediate frames; reflection/window/metal/portrait/silhouette risks inspected.
6. Motion: one action, one camera behavior, tested lead-angle range; no broad turns/orbit/face transitions; identify worst-case intermediate frame risk before generation.
7. Performance: actual measured guide/final timing; response never conclusively before its trigger; natural pause and listening; no mechanical identical order/eye duration imposed on all five scenes.
8. Light: plausible source, direction, softness, exposure and reflected fill; each angle belongs to the same space; lighting choice acknowledged as art rather than source-verified if appropriate.
9. Sound: per visible contact and selected material, not general character stereotype; exact edit-relative onset only after final frame contact exists; subtle cloth/breath/hand/floor sound and meaningful silence.
10. Provider/request: route/model live schema, input roles, image count, duration/resolution/frame constraints, exact request hash, existing scope and live balance, no undocumented trial flag.
11. Output: persisted provider ID and actual/estimated charge basis, local original and processed versions, technical decode and metadata, frame/sound/sync review, candidate/accepted/rejected disposition scoped to use.
12. Handoff: complete chapter playback evidence, gaps by time/frame, associated decisions, cost state, accepted next owner or specific blocker. Never infer acceptance from job status/hook completion.

## 7. Management code: existing protection and missing enforcement

Source observations, not a new penetration test:
