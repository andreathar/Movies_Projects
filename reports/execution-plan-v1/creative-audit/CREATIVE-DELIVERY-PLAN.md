## 5. Repeatable per-shot execution packet and gates

Each row of `shot-readiness-41.csv` must eventually have a packet with the following fields. The packet is prepared locally first and versioned; exact output paths should be chosen by the producer in an assigned future revision, not invented as existing artifacts.

1. **Identity:** episode; canonical slot ID(s); proposed production take ID; edit-event ID if split/reuse; intended edition(s); parent T-task; one named owner and reviewer; allowed files.
2. **Authority:** direct/recorded source of assignment; permitted activity; relevant decision IDs with exact artifact scope; new likeness/historical-framing questions; applicable paid scope status; hard stop condition. A planning packet is not submission authority.
3. **Immutable inputs:** file paths/hashes/version; real/synthetic/style/archival categories; continuity/art decisions; source text/line IDs; scene/reference fingerprint; old take references for possible reuse; provider reference roles if later needed.
4. **Dramatic purpose:** question/answer/reaction/residue; exact difficult line/word; intentional microreaction vs conclusion; emotional state before/after; why the shot earns its screen time.
5. **Spatial diagram:** table and chosen landmarks; participant seats; camera side/height/lens proposal; subject size; eyelines; offscreen areas; frame restriction; image crop rule; room light source positions. Numbers remain proposals until established in the accepted master.
6. **Safe movement:** one main action; one camera behavior; head/torso/hand extremes; in/out handles; prohibited surfaces/profile boundaries; mouth-visible intervals; contact points; no reverse shot or unforeseen orbit.
7. **Audio timing:** actual file/hash/version; transcript; start/end of phrases; pause and silence handles; trigger sample/timecode; proposed cut in/out; original versus retimed duration. Never accelerate visible speech to force a slot.
8. **Reference route:** exact first/last/multi-reference capability and declared input count; explain each reference role; no second-reference=OUT assumption. Existing compatible accepted route is reused, and any route change has a bounded test hypothesis.
9. **Cost/release:** live balance/capabilities at the applicable future preflight; exact request/schema/parameters; shot cap and remaining chapter reserve; estimated vs actual basis; single producer; submit-once intent persistence. An undecided fee blocks only that paid operation.
10. **Ingestion:** raw response, stable job ID, every output count, local untouched original/hash, downloads and accounting evidence; ambiguity stays unknown and cannot trigger automatic retry.
11. **Technical QC:** correct video stream selected by codec type; actual duration/frame count/resolution/fps/timebase/audio layout; full decode; first/last handles; all joins after normalization. Technical success is separate from art acceptance.
12. **Temporal picture QC:** full-frame sequential coverage including final partial frame batch; readable crop supplements; no historical face/reflection/portrait; Andre likeness/teeth/beard/jaw/gaze stability; fingers/anatomy; table/chair/wardrobe/light coherence; exact defect frame ranges. Sampled contact sheets are triage.
13. **Sound/sync QC:** listen at normal speed; actual words compared to source; transcript aids not substitutes for ear; articulation around start/end/pause; meaningful breaths; correct conclusive reaction timing; visible contact vs sound; room continuity and intelligibility.
14. **Disposition:** accepted within scope, rejected with defect/hypothesis, or pending exact missing gate. Frame-only evidence stays accepted_pending_full_playback. Andre's new likeness approval and final voice-by-ear approval remain separate.
15. **Integration and successor:** producer verifies output hashes/checks, compares concurrent source hashes, updates actual take/edit mapping and accounting, then obtains named successor acceptance or records a blocker with owner/unblock evidence. Completion event alone never means next work started.

Reusable film shot classes need additional tests: speaking OTS (lip sync plus shared-space/lead likeness); neutral listening (gaze and no stray mouth speech); historical eyes (tight crop through every frame); historical hands (finger count/contact/motion, no chin reflection); feet (sole/floor/hem/under-table geometry); rear hair (fixed head/no profile/reflection); table transition (same ring/chip/crop/light change motivated); legacy/archival insert (exact text, rights, date, crop and truthful context); still animation (explicit intentional visual-language approval and non-face geometry); opening/ending (hinge, notices and credits).
