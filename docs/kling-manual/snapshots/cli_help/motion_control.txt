Usage: kling motion_control --model <model> --image <url|path> (--video <url|path> | --motionId <id>) [options] [prompt]
Client options:
  --image            Subject image (required; local files auto-uploaded)
  --video            Motion-source video; mutually exclusive with --motionId
  --model            Model name (required; from kling who_am_i)
  --poll             Wait up to N seconds for results (0=off; bare flag=60)
Generation parameters are declared by who_am_i and may be passed as --<name>.
In an interactive terminal, missing required values are prompted before any upload.

MCP tool: motion_control

Description:
Generate a video by applying an uploaded video or saved motion to a subject image. Call who_am_i first to discover models and their exact argument/input specifications. Provide the required image plus exactly one motion source: a video input or a motionId from motion_library_list. Returns a generationId immediately; poll query_tasks for the result.
