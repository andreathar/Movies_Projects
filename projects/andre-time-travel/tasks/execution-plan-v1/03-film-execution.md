# Film execution: every chapter, shot and delivery gate

The detailed source-backed creative appendix is [CREATIVE-DELIVERY-PLAN.md](../../../../reports/execution-plan-v1/creative-audit/CREATIVE-DELIVERY-PLAN.md). Its [41-slot inventory](../../../../reports/execution-plan-v1/creative-audit/shot-readiness-41.csv), [22-line inventory](../../../../reports/execution-plan-v1/creative-audit/line-readiness-22.csv) and [16-legacy-insert inventory](../../../../reports/execution-plan-v1/creative-audit/legacy-readiness-16.csv) are part of this plan's evidence. They are dated planning views, not acceptance records. This chapter specifies how to execute those demands without losing their dependencies.

## 1. Preserve the story while making its timing executable

The canonical script and dialogue CSV govern text. There are 22 lines: opening and ending plus four lines per encounter. Existing scratch covers all 22 lines with a recorded total of 120.80 seconds, leaving time for questions, pauses, reactions, transitions and silence within the nominal 240-second cut. That total does not prove a convincing performance or a finished timeline.

Create one beat sheet per encounter with exact line ID, speaker, desire, personal perspective, factual anchor, pre-line emotion, trigger word/phrase, reaction onset, reaction consequence, mouth visibility, silence and cut handles. Keep Andre speaking from his own experience; avoid turning personal reputation into a universal historical verdict. Historical speech is sparse and fictional. Humor is permitted, but accidental anachronisms must be reviewed.

The nominal chapter slots are 4/10/4/8/4/7/5 seconds. Treat these as the current planned total, not a reason to accelerate speech or truncate a meaningful pause. If measured performance needs a change, reconcile manifest, shot list, atlas source and timeline together through the producer. The older six-beat framework and fixed eye durations require explicit disposition against the later dramatic direction. Two seconds is a Galileo candidate choice, not the duration of every reaction in the film.

## 3. The selected-shot packet

Create one versioned packet for each proposed production take, mapping it to one or more canonical edit slots. A provider job can yield multiple outputs; one output can serve several compatible edit events. Never conflate those quantities.

| Field group | Required contents |
|---|---|
| Identity and responsibility | Episode, canonical slot(s), local packet ID, intended editions, owner/session, independent reviewer, allowed output paths |
| Authority | Assignment reference, permitted actions, exact decision/approval scopes, unresolved personal gates and stop conditions |
| Inputs | Paths, hashes, versions and real/synthetic/style/archival categories; selected research/bible; source line/audio IDs; proposed reuse |
| Intent and causality | Before/after emotion, difficult phrase, trigger time, gesture onset, consequence and why the shot is needed |
| Space | Table anchors, chair/seat relationships, camera side, eyeline, light direction and crop-safe areas; dimensions labeled proposed until established |
| Motion | One principal action, restrained camera, start/end handles, forbidden angles/reflections, extremities and mouth-visible intervals |
| Audio | Exact guide/final track and hash, measured phrase/pause timing, contact frames, planned dubbing/sync strategy |
| Provider route | Exact installed-CLI command family and live schema, permitted reference count/roles, tested Element binding, actual output settings |
| Cost | Approved request fingerprint, provider-specific cap, remaining chapter/reserve allocation, live before/after readings, estimate-versus-actual basis |
| Ingestion | Durable submission intent, actual job ID/response, all output counts, preserved originals, downloaded hashes and charge record |
| QC | Full decode, streams/duration/fps; every risky frame/reflection; normal-speed audio playback; lips/words/causality/contact/light checks |
| Disposition and successor | Accepted/rejected/pending scope, exact evidence/defects, unchanged-source comparison, producer integration and next accepted owner |

The packet is ready for submission only when its applicable paid and creative gates pass. Preparation is free local work. New provider access, external audio fees and changed reference routes are evaluated only when necessary; no paid setup probes.

## 4. Execution loop and repair loop

1. Reuse prior accepted evidence that still matches the exact inputs and envelope. Identify only the unproven delta.
2. Review stills independently for historical material, likeness evidence, anatomy, axis/table/seat and light. The producer consolidates findings and creates only required corrections.
3. Present a small selected likeness packet to Andre. Earlier pilot likeness does not automatically approve every new still or angle.
4. Reconcile current bibles and measured guide timeline; build the English animatic using existing local audio. Final historical-language work can continue independently.
5. Prepare one exact request. Producer checks live schema/balance, applicable cap, reference roles, output settings and input hashes; persist intent before submission.
6. Submit once through the installed CLI. Immediately persist the response/job ID. If submission outcome is uncertain, reconcile the original request; never invent an ID or generate again to resolve uncertainty.
7. Resume/query the known job through the CLI, preserve originals and receipts, and review the entire actual clip.
8. Inspect every frame of shots that can reveal a historical face, including reflections and intermediate motion. Check Andre's teeth/jaw/beard/eyes, hand anatomy, table contact, cloth and light. A still-safe image is not a motion-safe clip.
9. Play normally with audio for sync, actual words, timing, gesture/contact and pacing. A decode, frame sheet or report cannot replace this.
10. Producer records acceptance only for the intended use and evidence scope. A pending-playback take remains pending.
11. Assemble a complete chapter; verify full playback and continuity across joins. Galileo proves the first complete recipe. Other chapters still require their own changed-context review.
12. If a defect remains, identify exact shot/frame/time range, one corrective hypothesis and least-cost route: edit, audio conform, source replacement or newly scoped generation. Rerun only affected checks and dependent joins.

## 5. Audio, dubbing and localization

Listen to the four existing opening voice tests before producing replacements. Keep native Kling audio as guide under D14; do not mark final voice approved from picture acceptance. Decide between Andre recording and a scoped approved clone route using actual sample judgment. Export dry lines with stable IDs and separate speaker/stem files, measure duration and preserve pronunciation notes.

For visible speech, changing the voice file does not guarantee synchronization. Conform pauses and wording, test a real representative difficult historical-language line, or use deliberate offscreen/L/J-cut coverage. Do not stretch facial speech to hide mismatches. A language model's self-review is not a qualified pronunciation review.

The language matrix must name target, historical approximation, reviewer, capability evidence, translation version, back-translation, pronunciation findings and accepted adaptation. Edition E can finish independently of target-selection research; Edition H cannot be called complete while a required language is pending. Both speakers' intended language behavior must match the chosen edition. Oppenheimer's English context does not exempt his chapter from caption, voice or sync review.

For foley, turn cue specifications into real files only for selected footage. Each cue identifies contact frame, floor/cloth/wood material, perspective, source/rights, level intent and silence around it. Felt, rug and planks are not interchangeable. Preserve dialogue, foley, ambience/room tone and optional music separately.

## 6. Full-film finishing and acceptance

Normalize sources into a new revision; retain the highest accepted originals. Resolve every edit slot to real local media, with source in/out and any reuse documented. Review complete English rough cut before spending reserve. Prioritize likeness, forbidden-face exposure, synchronization and story coverage before cosmetic polish. Freeze final dialogue timing before final picture lock, then conform color/light and mix.

Chosen delivery targets are **16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps, H.264/yuv420p and compatible AAC audio**. They are not claims about provider source resolution. Proposed web mix target is approximately **−16 LUFS integrated, true peak at or below −1 dBTP**, measured on the actual full exported file. Upscaling is not identity repair.

For both editions independently:

- Export versioned local master, captions/subtitles and stems. Record byte hash, duration and stream metadata.
- Decode the whole file and inspect joins, first/last frames, frame-rate conversion and subtitle timing/encoding.
- Review all risky frames and reflections, including transition frames and the final partial review batch.
- Play the exact exported file at normal speed with audio. Record reviewer, version, date, covered interval, equipment/surface limitations, findings and disposition.
- Obtain qualified language and caption review where required, and Andre's exact-file likeness, voice, performance and story acceptance.
- Package credits/fictionalization notice, research/rights sources, ledger, provenance and restoration instructions. Provider URLs alone do not constitute delivery.
- Producer updates final state only when both masters and all required gates are evidenced. `manage.py validate` alone currently cannot prove these conditions.

## 7. Preservation and continuation

Keep raw photographs, synthetic variants, original provider outputs, accepted/rejected takes, source snapshots, receipts, decisions and prior atlases. Logical retirement of superseded selections is preferred to moving media referenced by old handoffs. Include Git LFS objects in restoration planning and check that restored media are actual bytes, not pointer files.

A new episode begins only when requested, using a clean draft. Reuse principles and templates; choose a new brief, timing, encounters, languages, references, budget and approvals. Never inherit jobs, accepted takes, live-balance assumptions or production authorization. A completed episode may legitimately close with no successor.
