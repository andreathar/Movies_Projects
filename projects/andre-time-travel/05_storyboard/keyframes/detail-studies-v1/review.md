## Composition decisions and limits

- Use the left-shoulder view of each encounter as the principal composition candidate: Andre is screen-left looking camera-right, consistent with D09. The right-shoulder images place him screen-right; they remain axis studies and are **not selected for the current cut**. Do not intercut them automatically or mirror Andre. Their existence does not amend D09.
- Hands, feet and rear views are coverage options, not five mandatory cuts per chapter. Choose the minimum that makes the difficult line and reaction legible.
- Historical hands are fictional casting and gestures. Some thumbs/fingers are naturally occluded; anatomy and contact must be checked through motion before acceptance. No illness or biographical tic is asserted.
- Table ring/chip are visible in several new studies, but exact physical position and geometry are not certified across independently generated angles. Reconcile with D04 and the selected shared table shot before editing a sequence. Earlier Galileo hand crops do not establish exact table identity.
- Shared room, chair, table height, wardrobe seams and light positions are not yet proven continuous across all angles. Left/right alternatives can contain layout drift. New D20 clothing variants must not be inserted into old footage without a continuity pass.
- Andre’s real approved photo was supplied for new shoulder views along with the scene reference. The output remains synthetic: final likeness, expressions and performance belong to Andre’s review.
- No image here proves a safe head-turn envelope, lip sync, a timed reaction, audio synchronization or a final movie. Keep rear heads fixed and check every frame of later video. Do not assume any two images are a first/last-frame pair; only the earlier Galileo hand pair was proposed as a pair, and remains untested.
