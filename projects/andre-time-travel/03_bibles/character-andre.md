## 3. Camera Envelope & Screen Axis

- **Screen Direction:** Andre is positioned screen-left, facing **camera-right** (approx. 15° to 35° off lens axis).
- **Framing Safe Zone:** Medium Close-Up (MCU) from mid-chest to slightly above hair, and Seated Medium Shot (MS) showing waist, hands, and the rustic wooden table edge.
- **Forbidden Camera Motions:**
  - Zero 360° orbital camera moves.
  - Zero extreme rapid profile turns (>60° rapid pan).
  - Zero portal, morph, or glow effects over his face or hairline.
  - Zero hand movements obscuring the mouth or chin during dialogue segments.
- **Lighting Anchors:**
  - Soft directional key light from camera-left (wrap-around 45°).
  - Subtle architectural rim light separating his dark blazer from the moody background.
  - Color temperature adapts gently to each era's ambient plate (golden in Rome, amber hearth in Mongol tent, cool dusk in Arcetri, soft diffuse mist in St. Helena, tungsten noir in Trinity) while maintaining realistic, healthy skin tones.

## 4. Emotional Arc & Performance States

1. **Opening (Curiosity & Vulnerability):** Calm, contemplative arrival; gentle delivery of the opening monologue.
2. **Caesar (Respectful Reckoning):** Measured confidence meeting imperial authority; eye contact holds firm against ancient power.
3. **Genghis Khan (Quiet Gravity):** Respect for empire paired with unblinking acknowledgment of devastation and lost families.
4. **Galileo (Shared Intimacy & Defiance):** Deep empathy with the aged scholar; quiet intellectual solidarity at "Silencing a person does not settle a question."
5. **Napoleon (Complex Scrutiny):** Attentive scrutiny; refusing to grant unearned glory without weighing the human toll.
6. **Oppenheimer (Chilling Moral Weight):** Heaviness in the chest; solemn delivery of what happened at Hiroshima and Nagasaki.
7. **Ending (Introspective Return):** Solitary reflection back at the empty modern table; turning the dramatic inquiry inward.
