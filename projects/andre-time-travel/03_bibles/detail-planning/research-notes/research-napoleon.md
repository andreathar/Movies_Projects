# Napoleon at Longwood, autumn 1816 — material-culture evidence sheet (research notes)

Scope: back / below-neck / hands / feet only. No face evidence is used. Date of research: 2026-10-09. Nothing was written to the repo.

## How to read this sheet

Claim tags:
- **DOC** = documented about Napoleon himself (object tied to him, or a named witness describing him).
- **RANK** = what was normal for a man of his rank and period; not documented for him.
- **GENERIC** = generic Saint Helena / Georgian-Regency / tropical-island context; not about him.
- **NONE** = not found. Said plainly, with what is only generic.

Certainty grade (my own): **A** = contemporary eyewitness text, or a museum object with an inventory number, text fetched and read. **B** = retrospective memoir (written years later), or secondary book quoting an original, or press report of an object. **C** = inference from several sources.

Source status (see table at the end): **fetched-verified** = I read the text on the page or in the downloaded file and the quote is really there. **search-snippet-only** = only a search-engine summary; not read in full. **could not verify** = fetch failed or the claim is only in a search summary.

Quotes from archive.org scans are lightly cleaned of OCR noise. Where a date is inferred from page headers or entry order, I say so.

## Corrections to the brief (read first)

1. **"New rooms added Dec 1816": not found.** The sourced dates are earlier. Fondation Napoléon's plan (S13) says "In 1816, after Napoleon's arrival at Longwood, several more rooms were added", with the Montholons' drawing room, bedroom and bathroom "from July 1816". Lowe's own letter of July 1816 (S08) chose to add "new apartments" to Longwood instead of building a new house. Napoleon visited "Montholon's new rooms" that summer (S08). Lowe wrote on 27 Oct 1816 that "many parts of the house are still in an exceeding bad state" (S08). I found nothing dated December 1816. Treat "Dec 1816" as unsupported.
2. **"Autumn 1816" is northern-hemisphere wording.** Saint Helena is in the southern hemisphere, so Sept–Nov is spring there. The sources describe a damp, windy plateau; late October 1816 had six days of illness indoors, then "fair weather" on 31 Oct and "very fine" on 1 Nov (S01). Do not write "autumn leaves" or similar.
3. **The green Chasseur coat in 1816 is contested.** The Musée de l'Armée object sheet (S22) says he wore it "jusqu'en novembre 1815" and took it out again "en juillet 1820" per Lutyens' reports. Las Cases (April 1816) says he "laid aside his green uniform of the Guard" at Longwood and wore a hunting coat with the lace removed (S01). Fondation Napoléon's Sens dossier (S20) says he "continues to wear it" on the island. O'Meara in July 1816 (S02) and Clifford in Aug 1817 (S19) both describe a green coat. Net: in 1816 he wore a worn, turned green coat; whether it was the Chasseur colonel coat is **not settled**.
4. **Las Cases' room size looks wrong in the Gutenberg text.** The 1836 English text says two chambers "each 45 feet long and 42 broad, and about 7 feet high" (S01). O'Meara (May 1816) gives a bedroom "about fourteen feet by twelve, and ten or eleven feet in height" (S02), and Montholon gives rooms "12 feet by 15" and "9 feet high" (S04). I could not check the French original for the April 1816 passage. Use O'Meara / Montholon numbers; flag Las Cases' as a probable transcription or translation error.
5. **Ali's long room inventory is NOT 1816.** Chapter XIV of Saint-Denis (S06) describes rooms after the redecoration that followed the priests' arrival (1819) and the Montholons' departure. Moquette carpets, guillotine windows and a brass bed are 1819–20. For 1816 use Las Cases (April), O'Meara (May), Montholon (Dec 1815 state).

## Gaps / uncertainties

1. **Marchand's own *Mémoires* could not be read.** No English or French text is freely available. I verified only that the work exists and what the Fondation says of it (S25). Everything I attribute to "Marchand" here comes from other witnesses.
2. **Gourgaud's journal** (the full *Journal de Sainte-Hélène*) was not found as free text; the 1903 English "Talks" selection (S10) has almost no interior or dress detail. Bertrand's notebooks: not checked.
3. **Dec 1816 new rooms:** not found (see Corrections).
4. **Green Chasseur coat in 1816:** conflicting; Musée de l'Armée says not worn Nov 1815–July 1820 (S22).
5. **Collar, cuff and button details of the Sens coat:** not stated in the three official documents read. Needs the Sens inventory card or Déjean's restoration report.
6. **Footwear object evidence:** only the Fondation slippers (S11), gold stock buckle (S12), the Bata stockings (press report only, S16) and the Bertrand boots (press, S17). Malmaison shoe-buckle claim: could not verify. No object at Musée de l'Armée, RMG, BM or Sens found. Stocking colour conflicts (Bata: black; 1815 midshipman: white).
7. **Hair:** no queue, no "Titus" crop found. Grey vs dark brown conflicts in 1815 sources (Maitland: no grey; midshipman: turning grey). Colour of relics not given by the sources read. Barber after Dec 1816 unknown.
8. **Desk objects in 1816:** only Las Cases' trestle boards and marked writing table, a compass and pencil. Pewter ink, quills and a *Code Civil* volume: NONE.
9. **Ali's detail is 1819–20 or "general Longwood"**, written after 1821 and published 1922. Use as prop-style guidance only.
10. **Dimensions:** O'Meara's 14 × 12 × 10–11 ft bedroom is the best single figure for 1816. Montholon gives 12 × 15 and 9 ft; Las Cases' printed figure is implausible.
11. **OCR dates:** O'Meara dates (5 May, 14 May, 23 June, 26 July, Sept 1816 fire scene) are read from OCR page headers and entry order, not from the day line itself. Re-check against a page scan before quoting in the film bible.
12. **Several useful web pages could not be read** (Shannon Selin pages 403; napoleonexhibit.com 403; Science Museum object page 429; lestafette blog connection refused; Wikipedia Longwood House has nothing on 1816). Facts that came only from search summaries are marked in S28 and are not used as film facts above.
13. **Season wording** (see Corrections point 2).
