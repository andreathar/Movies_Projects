## 3. Back-view shot grammar (applies to all five chapters)

| Code | Shot | Frame & lens (full-frame equivalents, proposal) | Focus / blur level | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **BV-O** (phase 1, default) | Over-the-shoulder, **historical head completely outside the frame** | Position/lens in §3A (50 mm, ~115–120 cm, locked). Only the figure's near (LEFT) shoulder, upper arm, forearm on the near table edge and costume back edge enter from the right third. Head, neck, ear, hair, beard, headwear: outside the right/top edges. Andre centre-left, sharp. | **BL-3 (heavy)**: figure ~0.9–1.0 m from lens, Andre ~2.1 m; reads as soft shape + colour. | Main coverage for Andre's lines (speaking and listening keyframes from one master, as in the producer's pilot — fixes F01). |
| **BV-R** | Slow rack (same position as BV-O) | Start on the figure's sleeve/shoulder fabric (BL-1), rack to Andre. | **BL-1 → BL-3**: texture resolves **only below the neck** — the head is out of frame anyway. | Max once per chapter, on the line that names the legacy (line ID per chapter file). |
| **BV-W** (phase 2, only after BV-O passes QC) | Full-body back wide | Position/lens in §3A (35 mm, ~105 cm, further back). Whole seated body incl. the back of the head/headwear, chair legs and floor in the right third; Andre centre-left; whole table with stain + chip. | **BL-2 (medium)**: silhouette, headwear outline and garment blocks readable; no hair-strand detail. Head strictly from behind (§4). Higher face risk than BV-O → per-frame QC. | Establishes "he is at the table" (audit F02). Locked position for Route A dissolve. |
| **BV-H** | Hands/back insert (existing) | As v1 inserts. | Sharp allowed (below-neck, D05). | Unchanged from v1. |

Blur level definitions:
- **BL-3 heavy** — figure = shape and colour only. Safe for long takes.
- **BL-2 medium** — silhouette and costume construction readable; no facial-adjacent detail (ear rim may be visible; no cheek line).
- **BL-1 light** — fabric texture legible; allowed **only below the neck**.
- **BL-0 sharp on the head or any head-turn** — forbidden.

**Eyeline (D09-consistent):** Andre looks camera-right *toward the figure* — in BV-O toward the out-of-frame head position beyond the right edge (eyeline ~10–15° right of lens). The figure faces into the frame, away from the lens.

## 4. Face-safety rules for the back view (stricter than v1)

1. **Head rotation cap:** the figure's head may turn at most ~15° from "directly away from camera". No profile, no lost profile showing cheek contour, nose tip, beard outline, eyelashes or lips. If a generated take shows any of these in any frame → reject the take (not fixable by blur).
2. **No "turn to camera" motion** in any generated video; negative prompt every take: *turning head, looking back, profile, face visible, reflection of face*.
3. **Reflections:** no mirrors; no glossy surfaces in front of the figure (brass inkwell, candlestick, glass, window panes behind Andre at night act as mirrors — light them so they don't reflect, or keep them out of the figure's line); no wet-table sheen; Andre wears no glasses in these shots; no eye-reflection close-ups of Andre that could contain the figure.
4. **Shadows:** no hard key from the side that throws a profile shadow onto the wall/table. Key the figure from behind/above (rim), fill Andre from the front.
5. **Generation artefacts to watch:** AI models frequently (a) put a face on the back of the head, (b) rotate the head mid-clip, (c) mirror the body so the "back" becomes a front. QC must check every frame (continuity with `08_edit` QC; reviewers do not close tasks).
6. **Legacy intercuts obey the same rule:** portraits, statues, coins, banknotes and photographs that show the historical figure's face are **out** unless cropped below the neck, shot from behind, or defocused to BL-3. This includes the famous portraits used as costume sources in the per-figure files — they are *research references*, not on-screen material.
7. **Archival/real imagery:** any real photograph/artwork used on screen needs a rights check (producer). Generated imagery must not imitate a specific copyrighted photograph.

## 5. Legacy / repercussion intercut grammar (all chapters)

Purpose: while Andre speaks over the back view, intercut 2–4 short images per chapter answering three questions — **(A) what they became**, **(B) how they are spoken of today**, **(C) how their actions reverberated**.

Tone rules (intimate, not epic):
- Small, tactile, present-day images (a calendar page, a schoolbook, a museum vitrine, a document on a desk, a telescope in a backyard) rather than battle reconstructions.
- No on-screen invented text. Any legible text must be either (i) a verbatim, sourced quotation/inscription with a citation in the bible, or (ii) generic/out of focus.
- Every factual image carries a source ID in the per-figure file. Paraphrases are labelled **[paraphrase]**; verbatim quotations are labelled **[verbatim]** with source.
- The spoken dialogue remains `01_script/` (dialogue-sound role). Any line change suggested by these files is a **proposal for 01_script**, not an edit.

Suggested rhythm per chapter (fits the existing 42 s / 7-slot structure; editor to confirm): slots `x-02`, `x-04`, `x-06` (Andre's lines) → BV-O on Andre with 1 intercut each; `x-03`/`x-05` keep the approved eye crops (D05) or swap one of them for BV-W; `x-01` becomes BV-W establishing.
