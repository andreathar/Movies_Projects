## 2. Silhouette

1. **Dark academic gown/robe with a white collar standing out against it.** The Uffizi describes Sustermans's portrait (painted 1635, when "Galileo was around 70 years old and had been forced into exile in the countryside just out of Florence, on the hill of Arcetri" **[verbatim]**) with "the silky effect of the collar, which stands out white against the academic robes" **[verbatim]** [V2-G1].
2. **Rounded, heavier shoulders, slightly stooped** — age ~70 [V2-G1]; design interpretation of the portrait, not a documented posture.
3. **Grey/white hair and the outer edge of a full beard** may appear as a soft halo around the head at BL-3 only; the beard must never read as a jawline (no profile).
4. Armchair: a second Sustermans portrait (c. 1640) shows him seated in a chair [V2-G2] — supports a chair with arms; keep the backrest low.

## 4. Posture & seat continuity

- Baseline: settled deep in the chair, forearms on the armrests or one hand on the manuscript sheets (v1 insert; papers stay out of focus/unreadable).
- Slow; reactions are a lowering and lifting of the head on the neck axis (no turn) and the hand on paper.
- BV-O: Andre's eyeline goes to the out-of-frame head position beyond the right edge; BV-W: Andre's eyes sit just left of Galileo's head in frame.
- **Do not depict blindness in 1634.** His eyesight failed later (right eye by 1637, totally blind by December 1638, per the catalogue note cited in V2-G2) — consistent with v1's existing caution.
