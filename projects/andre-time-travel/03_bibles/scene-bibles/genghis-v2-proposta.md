## 2. Silhouette

1. **Leather warming cap** — the National Palace Museum (Taipei) notes "Genghis and Kublai wear a leather warming cap" **[verbatim]** in the Yuan imperial portrait album [V2-K1].
2. **Hair tied in braids**, visible behind the ears — "The three emperors shown here all have their hair tied in braids and are dressed in monochrome robes" **[verbatim]** [V2-K1]. From behind: braid loops at the nape/behind the ears = the clearest identifier.
3. **Monochrome robe**, wide-shouldered, heavy; seated heavy and grounded on the ~45 cm stool.
4. Age: about 65 in 1227 if born 1162 (Britannica gives born 1162, died August 18, 1227; also notes the birth date is uncertain — 1155, 1162 or 1167) [V2-K2]. Posture: heavy, weathered, not frail.

**Caveat (must stay attached to the costume choice):** no portrait from life survives; the Taipei album portrait is a later Yuan-dynasty image ("No contemporaneous portrait of Chinggis Khan has survived to this day" **[verbatim]**, Columbia Asia for Educators) [V2-K3]. It is the most authoritative visual source available, not a likeness.
