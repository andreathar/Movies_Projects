## 1A. Eye-cue placement rule — new direction (applies to every cue in §1 and §3)

Source of the direction: Andre via the dialogue-sound assignment of 2026-10-09 ("the eyes become a short reaction AFTER the figure is shown from behind"); consistent with the NEW CREATIVE DIRECTION in `memory/STATE.md` (2026-10-09 12:35) and with the editor+QC triage `08_edit/reviews/2026-10-09-revisao-nova-direcao/review.md` (eyes = one short beat, ~1.5–2 s, only after a back-view shot).

1. **Order: back view → eyes.** The figure's first presence in the chapter is always from behind (slot `x-01`). Each eye insert is immediately preceded by a **back-view beat** (≥ ~1.5 s) in which the figure's back/shoulder is the subject (BV-R / BL-1 shoulder, or BV-W in phase 2 — grammar in the figure's `-v2-proposta.md` §1 and `../table-and-continuity-v2-proposta.md`). Never cut to the eyes straight from Andre's close shot, and never open a chapter or a beat on the eyes.
2. **Short.** One eye insert = 1.5–2 s (hard max 2.5 s). Max two eye inserts per chapter (cue A, cue B).
3. **Trigger = what the eyes react to, not where they start.** The canonical trigger phrase lands over the back-view beat (or over BV-O); the eyes come *after* it, as the reaction. Andre's line may continue offscreen over the eyes (J-cut); the line is never cut or re-ordered to make room for the eyes.
4. **Holds and sustained stillness** in the canonical cue are played by the figure's back (body stops moving), not by lengthening the eye shot.
5. Unchanged: D05 eye bases, eyes-only safe crop, eyes screen-right with gaze camera-left (D09), zero full face/profile/reflection.

## 4. Historical Anchor & Research Citations

- **Exile & Setting:** Exiled to Saint Helena aboard HMS Northumberland in October 1815; resided at Longwood House from December 1815 until his death in May 1821.[4]
- **The Napoleonic Code:** Promulgated in 1804 as the *Code Civil des Français*, establishing legal equality and freedom of conscience while simultaneously institutionalizing severe patriarchal restrictions and curtailing women's legal rights.[4][17]
- **Linguistic Reality:** His childhood mother tongue was Corsican (an Italo-Romance dialect closely related to Tuscan); he spoke Italian before learning French at age 9 at Autun and Brienne, retaining an accent throughout his life.[8]
