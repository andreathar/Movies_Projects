## 2. Silhouette

1. **Extreme thinness.** NPS: "At five feet, ten inches (1.78 m) tall, he only weighed 115 pounds (52 kg) during his time directing Project Y" **[verbatim]** [V2-O1]. From behind: narrow shoulders, jacket hanging loose, thin neck — the strongest identifier after the hat.
2. **Porkpie hat.** LANL: "Oppenheimer often wore a brown porkpie hat (size 6 and 7/8, according to The New York Times)" **[verbatim]** [V2-O2]; AHF describes his "signature porkpie hat" at the Trinity site visit of September 1945 [V2-O3].
3. **Smoke.** NPS: he "chain smoked four to five packs of cigarettes a day along with a pipe" **[verbatim]** [V2-O1]. A thin smoke trail rising past the silhouette is sourced, visual and face-safe. (v1 already has unbranded cigarettes.)

## 6. Face-safety notes

- Hanging bulb overhead + window at night = mirror risk; keep the window behind Oppenheimer's back (i.e. behind camera) or curtained.
- Metal ashtray and pocket-watch: matte/turned away.
- Smoke can reveal a profile edge when back-lit — keep smoke **behind** the head plane relative to the key.
