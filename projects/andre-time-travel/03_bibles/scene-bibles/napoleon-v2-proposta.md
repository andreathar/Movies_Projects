## 2. Silhouette

1. **The hat.** Fondation Napoléon: "For most people, Napoleon and his hat are one"; he wore it "'en bataille', that is, with the corns [sic] parallel to shoulders" **[verbatim]**, unlike most officers. "Only four were taken to St Helena" **[verbatim]** [V2-N1]. From behind, a bicorne worn parallel to the shoulders is the single most readable Napoleonic silhouette.
   - **Recommendation:** the hat rests on the table beside him (indoors, at his own table), defocused but unmistakable; in BV-W it can be worn only if Andre/director want the stronger read (decision §8).
2. **Coat:** the green Chasseur colonel coat with its tails (he wore this jacket "if he went out", with the red sash and Legion of Honour plaque) [V2-N2].
3. **Everyday Longwood dress** was paler: a cream piqué waistcoat, casimir trousers, shirt, "a smoking jacket, and red leather slippers" **[verbatim]** [V2-N2]. This gives a sourced alternative for an *intimate* version (see §3).
4. Build/age: 46–47 in 1816 (born 15 August 1769, Ajaccio — **r3: verified**, Britannica "Napoleon I" https://www.britannica.com/biography/Napoleon-I; died 5 May 1821 — registry 4). Avoid height jokes/myths; no source used here for stature.
