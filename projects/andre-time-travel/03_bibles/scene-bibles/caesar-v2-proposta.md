## 2. Silhouette (how we know it is Caesar from behind, out of focus)

1. **Toga mass over the LEFT shoulder and arm**, the right arm free — the characteristic Roman draping (the Met's togatus: "a tunic and over it a toga, the most characteristic Roman dress") [V2-C2].
2. **Laurel wreath** seen from behind (ties/leaf tips at the nape): Suetonius reports that of all honours "there was none which he received or made use of more gladly than the privilege of wearing a laurel wreath at all times" **[verbatim, Rolfe translation]** [V2-C1, §45].
3. **Thin hair at the crown combed forward** — Suetonius: "he used to comb forward his scanty locks from the crown of his head" **[verbatim]** [V2-C1, §45]. From behind this reads as a thinning crown under the wreath. Keep subtle; no caricature.
4. **Backless stool** exposes the whole back drape (identity read without the face).
5. Build: Suetonius describes him as "tall of stature … shapely limbs" **[verbatim]** [V2-C1, §45] — upright, long-backed silhouette, not heavy.

## 4. Posture & seat continuity

- Baseline: upright, weight slightly forward, LEFT forearm resting on the table edge under the toga fold, right hand free (stylus/tablet per v1 props).
- He never turns toward camera; any reaction is played through shoulders (a slight settling/lift) and the right hand.
- Stool position: centre line opposite Andre; seat front 25–30 cm from the table edge; stool legs visible in BV-W.
- Age reference: early 44 BCE, mid-50s (Suetonius §88: "He died in the fifty-sixth year of his age" **[verbatim]** [V2-C1]).
