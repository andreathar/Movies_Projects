# Table and Continuity Bible

> **STATUS: PROPOSED** — nothing in this file is approved unless it cites a decision ID in `00_brief/decisions/`. Decided so far: D01 identity set, D02 wardrobe, D03 grooming, D04 table + travel rule, D05 eye bases, D09 screen axis. Likeness approval (pilot) remains Andre's.


**Working Title:** *The Table Between Centuries*  
**Core Motif:** The Plain Unpainted Wooden Table  
**Dramatic Role (decision D04):** The table is **Andre's**. It travels with him. The ring stain and the near-corner chip exist in every era because they are the same table — this is the only time-travel rule the film states, and the imagery states it. Locked anchor: `02_references/table/R1-J26-table-anchor_2.png`. Match cuts happen on the **insert** (hand + stain + chip, locked lens height), never on the wide; the seat opposite is era-specific, the table never is.

---

## 1. Table Physical Specifications

| Element | Specification & Visual Invariants |
|---|---|
| **Material** | Solid rustic unpainted European oak, natural matte finish (no gloss, no modern polyurethane coating). |
| **Surface Detail** | Pronounced longitudinal wood grain running parallel to the long edge, subtle natural cracks, soft weathered patina, visible plane marks, historic hand-hewn imperfections. |
| **Edge Geometry** | Squared front edge with gentle 5mm bevel; natural softened corner distressing. |
| **Dimensions in Frame** | Standard dining/communion height (approx. 75cm). Table surface occupies the lower third to lower half of medium seated shots. |
| **Continuity Anchors** | The wood grain pattern and the distance between Andre's seated torso and the front table edge remain identical across all eras. |

---

## 2. The Match-Cut Mechanism

The transitions between eras **never morph Andre's face** or use portal/sci-fi visual effects. Instead, transitions are executed as **camera and physical match cuts anchored to the table**:

1. **Shot A (Departure):** Andre's hand rests on the rustic oak table grain; the camera holds on the hand and the edge.
2. **Audio Bridge:** The sound of the current era (e.g., wind through Arcetri trees or dripping rain in Trinity) bleeds across the cut.
3. **Cut Point:** A sharp or motivated cut to the identical framing on the table surface in the new setting.
4. **Shot B (Arrival):** The light and era-specific props have transformed around the table, but the table itself remains unchanged.

## 4. Continuity Invariants & Forbidden Practices

- **Zero Full Faces of Historical Figures:** In every frame, historical figures are strictly seen via extreme close-up eye crops (brow to lower eyelid) or below-neck attire/hands. No reflections in polished surfaces, glass, water, or shadows may reveal facial contours.
- **Screen Axis:** Andre is always seated screen-left, looking camera-right. The interlocutor's eyes are always screen-right, looking camera-left.
- **Table Integrity:** The table must never bend, morph, or change wood species during generation.
