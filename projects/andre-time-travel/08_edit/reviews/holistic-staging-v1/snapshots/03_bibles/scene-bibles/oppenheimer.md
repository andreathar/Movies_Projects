## 1. Dramatic Progression & Emotional Arc

- **Core Question:** *Can technical success answer a moral question?*
- **Oppenheimer's Motivation:** (Dramatic invention, not documented behaviour) High anxiety in the stormy desert night before the unprecedented release of atomic energy; seeking confirmation that the weapon will function and bring an end to world war.
- **Eye Cue Progression (Eyes-Only Safe Crop):**
  - **Cue A (`O-03`, 4s):** *Restless feverish anticipation draining completely* into hollow shock as Andre names Hiroshima and Nagasaki. Pupils dilate slightly; the breath catches.
  - **Cue B (`O-05`, 4s):** *Haunting moral stillness and absolute dread* at "I can't tell you that." Near-motionless gaze, staring into the dark abyss of the future.

## 4. Historical Anchor & Research Citations

- **Trinity Test Context:** July 16, 1945 at about 5:30 a.m. at the Trinity site, Alamogordo Bombing and Gunnery Range, New Mexico.[5]
- **Subsequent Bombings:** Hiroshima on August 6, 1945 and Nagasaki on August 9, 1945.[18][19] Japan's surrender broadcast 15 August; formal ceremony 2 September 1945.[20]
- **Contemporary Ethical Debate:** Documented discussions among Manhattan Project scientists (Franck Committee, Interim Committee) regarding the use, demonstration, and future international control of nuclear weapons.[9]
