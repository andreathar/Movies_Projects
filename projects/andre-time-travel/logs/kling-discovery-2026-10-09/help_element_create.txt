Usage: kling element_create --name <name> --description <text> --tag <tag> [options]
Resource options (URLs or local paths; local files auto-uploaded):
  --cover <file>       Main image for an image element
  --secondary <file>   Secondary image (repeat 1-3 times)
  --video <file>       Video subject (instead of cover/secondary)
  --voice <file>       Optional .mp3 voice
Use either --video, or --cover plus 1-3 --secondary values. --tag is repeatable.

--- Live server declaration (tools/list) ---
MCP tool: element_create

Description:
Create a new Kling Element (reusable subject / character). Image subject: cover image plus 1–3 secondary images. Video subject: a single video URL in resource.video (do not pass cover/secondary). Both accept an optional user-uploaded .mp3 voice URL and require at least one tag. Choose tag name(s) from the tag catalog in this description: Characters, Animals, Items, Costumes, Scenes, Effects, Others. Image/video/voice URLs must be publicly accessible (upload local files via file_upload first). Video subject constraints (enforced downstream): Duration 3–60s; fps > 23.8; short edge ≥ 700px; resolution ≤ 3840×2160 (4K); SAR must be empty or 1:1 or 0:1; HDR (HDR10/HLG/HDR10+/Dolby Vision) not supported.. Where a subject can be used: Image subjects ARE allowed on image_to_image (INCLUDING kling-image-o1) and on image_to_video models that list 'elements'. Do not say kling-image-o1 cannot use image subjects. Video subjects are allowed ONLY on image_to_video with kling-video-v3_0_omni or kling-video-v3_0 — not on image_to_image, not on kling-image-o1, not on kling-video-o1, not on text_to_image / text_to_video. text_to_image and text_to_video cannot use any subjects. If the user's intended tool/model cannot use that subject type, tell them first and pick a compatible one. Returns the element id.
