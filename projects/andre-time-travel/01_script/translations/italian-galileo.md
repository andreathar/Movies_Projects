## 1. Dialogue Script & Phonetic Transcription

### Line G-L01
- **Speaker:** Galileo (Off-Screen)
- **English Original:** *"Am I finally believed?"*
- **17th-Century Tuscan Italian:** **"Son io finalmente creduto?"**
- **IPA Phonetics:** /ˈson ˈi.o fi.nalˈmen.te kreˈdu.to/
- **Phonetic Guide:** *Sohn EE-oh fee-nahl-MEN-teh kray-DOO-toh?*
- **Literal Gloss:** Am I finally believed?

### Line G-L02
- **Speaker:** Andre (Seated, Facing Camera-Right)
- **English Original:** *"People learn from you. They also turn you into a simple story: one scientist against a whole world. The truth is harder than that."*
- **17th-Century Tuscan Italian:** **"Gli uomini imparano da voi. Ma vi riducono anche a una favola semplice: un solo filosofo contro l'intero mondo. La verità è assai più aspra."**
- **IPA Phonetics:** /ʎi ˈwɔː.mi.ni imˈpaː.ra.no da ˈvoi. ma vi riˈduː.ko.no ˈan.ke a ˈuː.na ˈfaː.vo.la ˈsem.pli.tʃe: un ˈsoː.lo fiˈlɔː.zo.fo ˈkon.tro linˈtɛː.ro ˈmon.do. la ve.riˈta ɛ asˈsai pju ˈas.pra/
- **Phonetic Guide:** *Lyee WOH-mee-nee eem-PAH-rah-noh dah VOY. Mah vee ree-DOO-koh-noh AHN-keh ah OO-nah FAH-voh-lah SEM-plee-cheh: oon SOH-loh fee-LOH-zoh-foh KOHN-troh leen-TEH-roh MOHN-doh. Lah veh-ree-TAH eh ahs-SY PYOOH AHS-prah.*
- **Literal Gloss:** The men learn from you. But you they-reduce also to a fable simple: one single philosopher against the-entire world. The truth is far more harsh.

### Line G-L04
- **Speaker:** Andre (Seated & Audio Overlap on Table Cut)
- **English Original:** *"Your observations. And the warning in your trial. Silencing a person does not settle a question."*
- **17th-Century Tuscan Italian:** **"Le vostre osservazioni. E l'ammonimento del vostro processo. Far tacere un uomo non risolve la controversia."**
- **IPA Phonetics:** /le ˈvɔː.stre os.ser.vaˈtsjoː.ni. e lam.mo.niˈmen.to del ˈvɔː.stro proˈtʃɛs.so. far taˈtʃeː.re un ˈwɔː.mo non riˈsɔl.ve la kon.troˈvɛr.sja/
- **Phonetic Guide:** *Leh VOH-stray ohs-sehr-vah-TSYOH-nee. Eh lahm-moh-nee-MEN-toh del VOH-stroh proh-CHES-soh. Fahr tah-CHAY-reh oon WOH-moh nohn ree-ZOHL-veh lah kohn-troh-VEHR-syah.*
- **Literal Gloss:** The your observations. And the-warning of-the your trial. To-make silent a man not resolves the dispute.

## 2. Dubbing & Lip-Sync Editorial Notes

- Tuscan phonetics use the historic formal address (*voi* instead of modern *lei*), which was standard for respected scholars in 17th-century Italy.
- The phrase *far tacere un uomo non risolve la controversia* is phonetically rhythmic and maps cleanly onto Andre's seated delivery.
