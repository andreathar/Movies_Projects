## 1. Dialogue Script & Phonetic Transcription

### Line C-L01
- **Speaker:** Caesar (Off-Screen)
- **English Original:** *"Does my name survive?"*
- **Classical Latin:** **"Superestne nomen meum?"**
- **IPA Phonetics:** /ˈsu.pe.rest.ne ˈnoː.men ˈme.um/
- **Phonetic Guide:** *SOO-peh-rest-neh NOH-men MEH-oom?*
- **Literal Gloss:** Survives-interrogative name my?

### Line C-L02
- **Speaker:** Andre (Seated, Facing Camera-Right)
- **English Original:** *"Your name survives. So do the reforms and the victories people study. But they also study how much power one man can hold—and what happens to a republic around him."*
- **Classical Latin:** **"Nomen tuum superest. Atque instituta et victoriae quae homines discunt. Sed etiam discunt quantum imperium unus homo capere possit—et quid rei publicae circum eum accidat."**
- **IPA Phonetics:** /ˈnoː.men ˈtu.um ˈsu.pe.rest. ˈat.kʷe in.stiˈtuː.ta et wikˈtoː.ri.ae̯ kʷae̯ ˈho.mi.neːs ˈdis.kunt. sed ˈe.ti.am ˈdis.kunt ˈkʷan.tum imˈpe.ri.um ˈuː.nus ˈho.moː ˈka.pe.re ˈpos.sit—et kʷid rejiː ˈpuːb.li.kae̯ ˈkir.kum ˈe.um ˈak.ki.dat/
- **Phonetic Guide:** *NOH-men TOO-oom SOO-peh-rest. AHT-kweh in-stee-TOO-tah et week-TOH-ree-eye kwye HOH-mee-nays DEES-koont. Sed EH-tee-ahm DEES-koont KWAHN-toom eem-PEH-ree-oom OO-noos HOH-moh KAH-peh-reh POHS-seet—et kwid REH-yee POOB-lee-kye KEER-koom EH-oom AHK-kee-daht.*
- **Literal Gloss:** Name your survives. And institutions and victories which people study. But also they-study how-much power one man hold may—and what to-republic around him happens.

### Line C-L04
- **Speaker:** Andre (Seated & Audio Overlap on Table Cut)
- **English Original:** *"They argue about you. A brilliant commander. An ambitious ruler. I came to ask what victory leaves behind."*
- **Classical Latin:** **"De te contendunt. Imperatorem egregium. Dominum cupidum. Veni rogatum quid victoria post se relinquat."**
- **IPA Phonetics:** /deː teː konˈten.dunt. im.pe.raːˈtoː.rem eːˈgre.gi.um. ˈdo.mi.num ˈku.pi.dum. ˈweː.niː roˈgaː.tum kʷid wikˈtoː.ri.a post seː reˈlin.kʷat/
- **Phonetic Guide:** *Day tay kon-TEN-doont. Eem-peh-rah-TOH-rem ay-GREH-gee-oom. DOH-mee-noom KOO-pee-doom. WAY-nee roh-GAH-toom kwid week-TOH-ree-ah post say reh-LEEN-kwaht.*
- **Literal Gloss:** About you they-dispute. Commander brilliant. Ruler ambitious. I-came to-ask what victory behind itself leaves.

## 2. Dubbing & Lip-Sync Editorial Notes

- Classical Latin word lengths are roughly 10% longer in syllable count than English. Andre’s delivery must be measured, grave, and unhurried.
- If Edition H relies on external audio lip-sync on Andre's face, the `C-L02` and `C-L04` lines are segmented at natural phrase boundaries (`Nomen tuum superest... / Sed etiam discunt...`) to allow cutaways to Caesar's eye reactions and the tabletop.
